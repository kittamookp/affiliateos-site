AffiliateOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

AffiliateOS is currently in development and testing. This policy explains how information may be handled when users authorize supported third-party integrations.

Information We May Process

When a user authorizes a supported platform integration, AffiliateOS may process account identifiers, profile information, authorization data, publishing status, and content-related information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Authentication Data

Access tokens and other authentication credentials are used only to communicate with authorized third-party services and should not be publicly disclosed.

Use of Information

Information is used to provide and test AffiliateOS functionality, including content preparation, review, publishing, and performance analysis.

Third-Party Services

AffiliateOS may connect to services such as TikTok. Information processed by those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as reasonably necessary for development, operation, security, and troubleshooting of the service.
